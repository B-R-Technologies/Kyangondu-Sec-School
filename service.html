Clubs & Societies

At Kyang'ondu Mixed Secondary School, we believe that education goes beyond the classroom. Our wide range of clubs and societies gives students the opportunity to discover their talents, build leadership skills, and develop a strong sense of teamwork and responsibility.

Debate Club

Sharpening critical thinking, communication, and public speaking skills.

Science Club

Encouraging curiosity and innovation through experiments and projects.

Drama and Music Club

Nurturing artistic expression through performances and creativity.

Journalism Club

Developing writing, photography, and storytelling skills for the school newsletter.

Sports and Athletics

We recognize that sports and athletics are vital for the physical, mental, and social growth of our students. Through organized sporting activities, learners develop discipline, teamwork, resilience, and leadership — qualities that extend far beyond the field. Our sports program encourages every student to participate, whether for recreation, competition, or personal fitness. The school provides well-maintained sports grounds and equipment, ensuring that every learner has an opportunity to discover and nurture their athletic talents. We take pride in our students’ participation in interschool, sub-county, county, and national competitions, where they continue to represent the school with dedication and sportsmanship.

Hockey

Our team is built on teamwork, strategy, and skill. We learn precision & coordination as we compete in friendly matches and tournaments, proudly representing the school at different levels.

Football

Football is one of the most loved sports in our school. Both boys and girls teams train regularly and participate in interschool and regional competitions, showcasing passion and sportsmanship.

Tennis

Tennis offers students a chance to build focus, endurance, and quick reflexes through competitive drills and matches.
