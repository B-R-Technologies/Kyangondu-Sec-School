Kyang'ondu Secondary School Clubs and Societies

Our wide range of clubs and societies promotes holistic development at Kyang'ondu. They give students the opportunity to discover their talents, build leadership skills, and develop a strong sense of teamwork and responsibility. Through active participation, students learn to express themselves, serve their community, and grow in confidence while forming lifelong friendships.

Debate Club

Sharpening critical thinking, communication, and public speaking skills.

Science club

Encouraging curiosity and innovation through experiments and projects.

Drama and Music Club

Nurturing artistic expression through performances and creativity.

Journalism Club

Developing writing, photography, and storytelling skills for the school newsletter.
